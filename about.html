About Economic Funding Company™

A federated licensing and funding architecture supporting independent economic development authorities.

Who We Are

We are an independent, non-political not-for-profit. Our business activities, including revenue-generating activities, serve one purpose: creating and growing economic development and improving quality of life for residents of countries, states and municipalities.

Economic Funding Company™ (EFC™) provides the capital architecture, licensing framework, and standards that enable independent, locally owned economic funding entities to operate with consistency and credibility across jurisdictions.

Federated Model

Each sub-entity — such as California Economic Funding Company™, Ecuador Funding Company™, and others — is independently owned, locally recognized, and licensed. In each jurisdiction, the local entity is the public-facing authority. The parent architecture operates behind the scenes, ensuring structure, funding integrity, and long-term support.
